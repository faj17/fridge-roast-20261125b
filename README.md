One line roasting a smart fridge (103 characters):

> My fridge's firmware update bricked the ice maker, then it pushed a notification: 'Upgrade successful.'

The setup is a firmware update bricking the ice maker. The turn is the fridge pushing a notification about it. The punch is the notification itself: the fridge declares the upgrade a success, proud of the very update that broke it. No groceries ordered, nobody watched — just a gadget congratulating itself for breaking its own best feature.
